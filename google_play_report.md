## 应用简介

A sudden zombie outbreak has overrun our quiet frontier town, plunging it into chaos and terror. As the lone lawman in these parts, you - the Sheriff - choose to stand your ground become the last beacon of hope, protecting survivors, rebuilding shelters, and holding back the relentless undead hordes.

So dust off your cowboy hat, strap on that star, and show these walking corpses who truly rules the wild west!

〓Game Features〓

▶ Rebuild Border Town
Transform ruins into a thriving settlement. Upgrade buildings, fortify defenses, and make critical decisions that determine your town's survival in this post-apocalyptic wilderness.

▶ Recruit Specialized Survivors
Enlist unique characters - doctors, hunters, blacksmiths, and soldiers - each with vital skills. In this harsh world, talent means survival.

▶ Manage Survival Supplies
Assign survivors to farm, hunt, craft, or heal. Balance resources while monitoring health and morale. A true sheriff knows his people's needs.

▶ Repel Zombie Invasions
Prepare tactical defenses, train elite troops to fend off zombie waves. Face standard walkers and special mutations - each requiring unique counterstrategies.

▶ Explore the Wilderness
Venture beyond town limits into uncharted territory. Discover vital resources, unearth hidden caches, and forge alliances with other settlements. Every expedition balances risk and reward - only the boldest sheriffs return with the treasures their town needs.

▶ Form Powerful Alliances
In this merciless world, lone wolves perish quickly. Forge bonds with fellow sheriffs, share resources, provide mutual aid, and stand united against the undead hordes. Join alliance conflicts, seize critical resources, and establish your coalition as the dominant force in the wasteland.

▶ Develop Survival Technologies
Commit precious resources to scientific advancement. Unlock crucial survival technologies that transform your settlement’s capabilities. In this apocalyptic age, those who innovate survive - those who stagnate perish.

▶ Challenge the Arena
Lead your elite fighters into the blood-soaked arena. Test your tactical prowess against rival sheriffs, claim valuable prizes, and etch your name in wasteland legend. In this brutal new world, respect is earned through victory, and glory belongs to the strong.

In Dawn Watch: Survival, you’re not just a frontier sheriff - you’re the last symbol of hope, the shield of civilization itself. Are you ready to face the undead scourge, reclaim the lawless wastes, and restore order to the west?

Download now, strap on your badge, and carve your legend into this apocalyptic frontier. The dawn of justice begins with you.
